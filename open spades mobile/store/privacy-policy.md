# OpenSpades Mobile — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

OpenSpades Mobile ("the app") is a free, open-source game. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in or registration.
- No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- The developer does not receive, collect or store any personal information from the app.

## Information stored on your device

Your settings (player name, controls, graphics and sound preferences, last server address and filters) are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include these settings in your personal Google backup.

## Network connections

- **Server list:** the app downloads the public server list from Build and Shoot (`services.buildandshoot.com`) over HTTPS. That service sees your IP address, as with any web request.
- **Game servers:** when you choose to join a server, the app connects directly to that server. The server receives your IP address, the player name you chose, your in-game actions and any chat messages you send. Game servers are run by independent community operators, not by the developer; their own policies apply.
- **Links:** links in the Credits screen open in your web browser.

## Files you save

Screenshots and maps are saved only where you choose using Android's file picker.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Online servers include unmoderated chat with other players.
